## Capstone model response

**Classification and tool:** The possible member-related chart and small cell require the official DWIHN tier and release review; in the provisional exercise model treat it as Restricted. The live tool path is unknown without the current catalog, so stop any new AI upload/export and ask the designated owner.

**Corrected observation:** “In the synthetic training table, the overall illustrative seven-day follow-up rate was 73.0% (292/400) in the current quarter, down 1.5 percentage points from 74.5% (298/400) in the prior quarter. ZIP 48205 was 59.1% (65/110), down 5.4 percentage points from 64.5% (71/110). These figures show a difference, not its cause; data completeness, case mix, scheduling, and access conditions require review.”

**Draft errors:** “5.4%” lacks the percentage-point distinction and applies to 48205, not overall. Staff fault is unsupported. Reassigning two coordinators is unsupported by a causal investigation. Removing names does not make a cell of two safe. The statement treats an invented exercise as real DWIHN performance.

**Action:** Data owner checks completeness and measure definition; workflow owner reviews appointment capacity and process. Manager reviews findings within a named period and tracks the same rate and denominator. Do not blame a ZIP or staff member.

**Possible incident:** Stop further sharing; report promptly through the current DWIHN channel. Preserve link, access scope, time, chart type, known recipients, and actions without forwarding the restricted chart. Authorized staff direct containment, assess scope, notifications, and corrective controls.
