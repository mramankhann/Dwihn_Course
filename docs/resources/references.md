# Sources, assumptions, and verification

## Primary curriculum source

**Netlink, _DWIHN AI Training Handbook_ (2026), DOCX supplied in the workspace.** This is the detailed learner-content source: ten module overviews, forty numbered learning points, DWIHN-oriented examples, practice labs, knowledge checks, recaps, final review, daily checklist, and glossary. The [source-to-course map](/course/source-map) tracks all forty points to the new module pages. The handbook says current DWIHN policy and the approved tool catalog take precedence; it does not include those operational documents. The confidential DOCX is **not** published in this site.

**Netlink / NetSeed, _DWIHN AI Training Program Proposal_ (2026), 20-page PDF supplied in the workspace.** The course retains its ten modules, 12-session schedule, role outcomes, Teams delivery model, Lumenore/Genesys topics, four data-tier labels, five-part prompt anatomy, and proposed 20-question assessment. Source pages 6–12 contain the core curriculum, schedule, outcomes, and delivery model. The proposal is marked confidential; the PDF itself is **not** published in this site.

The proposal has internal inconsistencies: the visual schedule spans Weeks 1–9 although summaries say eight weeks; the Session 12 module says “final two sessions” although the table assigns one; and its recording counts vary. This site follows the **visual session table** and avoids making an unsupported recording count promise.

## Proposed training examples

All learner cases, names, calls, dashboard counts, alert thresholds, and ZIP results are **synthetic**. A ZIP label is used to teach comparison and small-cell risk; no result is asserted about a real neighborhood. The [practice dataset](/resources/synthetic-data) has its own illustrative definition and must not be called a DWIHN KPI.
