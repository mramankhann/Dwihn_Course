# Analytics review card

Use this checklist before citing an AI-generated chart, Lumenore answer, alert, or board report.

| Check | Ask |
|---|---|
| Decision | What decision is the analysis meant to support? |
| Definition | What exactly are the numerator, denominator, population, and exclusions? |
| Source | Which governed dataset and refresh time produced the view? |
| Period | Are dates and comparison windows aligned? |
| Filters | Which ZIP, program, service, or staff filters are active? |
| Magnitude | Is the change in counts, percent, or **percentage points**? |
| Stability | Is the subgroup large enough? Are small cells protected under DWIHN rules? |
| Alternatives | What else could explain the pattern? What would disprove our first guess? |
| Action | Who will do what by when, and which measure will show whether it worked? |
| Audience | May this person see the chart or export? Does the link have appropriate access? |

**Example:** In the invented exercise, a change from 74.5% to 73.0% is a decline of 1.5 **percentage points**. The cause remains unknown. [Synthetic dataset](/resources/synthetic-data) · [S5–S7](/sessions/s05-lumenore-ask-me)
