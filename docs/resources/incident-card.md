# AI incident response card

**For a suspected real incident, follow DWIHN's current procedure and report promptly through its designated channel.** This card is a proposed training aid; the proposal does not supply official contacts or a full incident policy.

![Five-step incident response](/diagrams/incident-flow.svg){.diagram}

| Step | Staff action |
|---|---|
| 1 · Stop | Stop the activity and prevent further sharing using approved instructions. Do not erase evidence independently. |
| 2 · Report | Contact the current DWIHN reporting channel or supervisor **immediately**. The proposal's 24-hour target is not a waiting period. |
| 3 · Record | Note time, tool/account, data type, destination, recipients if known, actions taken, and unknowns. Do not re-send sensitive data to explain it. |
| 4 · Follow direction | Privacy/security and tool owners assess, contain, and decide notifications or corrective steps. |
| 5 · Learn | Document the approved fix and a check that it works. Share sanitized lessons. |

## What may count as an incident?

An unapproved upload of PHI, a dashboard link shared too widely, an inaccurate AI summary entered as fact, unauthorized recording, permission error, or repeated unsafe recommendation. Report near misses as DWIHN policy requires.

HHS describes external breach notification obligations; staff should not self-determine those obligations from this card. [HHS Breach Notification Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/breach-notification/index.html) · [S11 tabletop](/sessions/s11-governance)
