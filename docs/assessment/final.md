# Final knowledge assessment

**20 questions ? Module 10**

Choose one best answer per question. Use the current DWIHN policy for real work; these questions assess course principles. This page deliberately does not display answers so the instructor can administer it. Pair it with the [capstone practical case](/projects/capstone). DWIHN approves final certificate criteria.

This assessment closes the handbook's [Module 10](/modules/module-10). Review the ten module takeaways and practical scenarios first; the questions ask you to apply ideas across the course rather than recall isolated wording.

## Questions

**1.** Which is a suitable AI-assisted task when the tool and data are approved?

A. Making a final clinical diagnosis  
B. Drafting a routine email for human review  
C. Determining crisis level without staff review  
D. Granting a member benefit automatically

**2.** A generated paragraph sounds confident. What does that prove?

A. The facts are current  
B. The source has been checked  
C. Only that the wording is fluent  
D. The task is approved by DWIHN

**3.** A product appears in an approved-tool list. What else must you confirm before entering data?

A. Only that you like its output  
B. The exact tenant, feature, purpose, role, and permitted data class  
C. Whether a coworker used it once  
D. Whether it has a mobile app

**4.** Which statement best reflects HHS guidance for a cloud service processing ePHI on a regulated organization's behalf?

A. A marketing claim makes it HIPAA-approved  
B. Encryption alone removes all business associate duties  
C. Applicable HIPAA requirements, including a BAA when required, must be addressed  
D. Any public chat tool may be used for PHI

**5.** A document includes a public paragraph and an identifiable member note. How should the input be classified for the training exercise?

A. By the public paragraph only  
B. By the highest tier present, then checked against DWIHN policy  
C. As Public after deleting the title  
D. By whichever tier permits the quickest task

**6.** Why can a ZIP-level chart with a cell of two still be sensitive?

A. Maps are always inaccurate  
B. Small groups combined with other facts can reveal people  
C. A chart is never shareable  
D. ZIP codes are secret

**7.** Which set names the course's five prompt parts?

A. Role, Task, Context, Format, Constraint  
B. Model, Speed, Length, Color, Tone  
C. Source, Chart, Export, Alert, Email  
D. Input, Output, Approval, Budget, Deadline

**8.** A needed appointment address is absent from the approved source. What should the prompt request?

A. Invent a plausible address  
B. Copy one from an unrelated website  
C. Mark the missing fact for human review  
D. Remove all constraints

**9.** An AI draft claims a member appointment was booked, but the source only says a callback was requested. What is the right review action?

A. Keep the claim because it sounds helpful  
B. Correct the claim and preserve the unresolved status  
C. Send it and fix it later if challenged  
D. Let the AI decide

**10.** What is required before sending an AI-translated member communication?

A. No review if the translation reads smoothly  
B. An approved workflow and qualified human review under DWIHN policy  
C. Only a long prompt  
D. Posting it to a public forum for feedback

**11.** Which Ask Me question is best scoped?

A. “How are we?”  
B. “Tell me something interesting.”  
C. “Show the current-quarter seven-day follow-up rate versus prior quarter by ZIP, with counts.”  
D. “Fix access.”

**12.** In the synthetic dataset, 292 of 400 qualifying cases have a completed follow-up. What is the rate?

A. 29.2%  
B. 73.0%  
C. 74.5%  
D. 108%

**13.** What should accompany a ZIP-level rate on a dashboard?

A. Only a bright color  
B. Numerator/denominator, definition, period, and privacy review  
C. A claim about the cause  
D. A ranking of staff

**14.** Which alert design is most useful?

A. A threshold with no owner or response  
B. An alert for every small fluctuation  
C. A defined measure, threshold, minimum volume, owner, response, and closure check  
D. A confidential export to everyone

**15.** A Genesys AI transcript changes “missed a call” to “lost an appointment.” What should staff do?

A. Trust the transcript over the interaction  
B. Correct the record after checking the interaction under policy  
C. Ignore all future transcripts  
D. Enter the invented appointment outcome

**16.** A negative sentiment flag means:

A. The caller has a diagnosed condition  
B. The call must be routed to a specific service regardless of content  
C. Staff should consider it a cue and apply the actual call and escalation protocol  
D. The summary is automatically accurate

**17.** Completed follow-ups double from 42 to 84 while eligible cases double from 60 to 120. What happens to the rate?

A. It doubles  
B. It halves  
C. It remains 70%  
D. It cannot be calculated

**18.** A forecast suggests a metric may decline. What is the most responsible next step?

A. Treat it as certain and make an automatic member-level decision  
B. Review data, uncertainty, alternatives, and a human-owned response  
C. Hide it from the team

A. Wait until the proposed 24-hour window ends  
B. Forward the chart to several people for advice
