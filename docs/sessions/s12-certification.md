# Session 12 · Assessment & Certification

**Week 9 · Module 10 · Assessment + Q&A + recognition · At least one hour; Q&A included**

## Handbook module reading

Read [Module 10 - Knowledge Assessment & Certification](/modules/module-10) before this session. The module page provides the topic introduction, four detailed learning points with examples, key takeaways, and a practical scenario. Use this session page for the live demonstration and guided practice.

## Purpose

This session consolidates Modules 1–9 and checks whether staff can make safe, useful decisions without prompting from the trainer. The source proposal calls for an online 20-question assessment, live Q&A, and a DWIHN AI Readiness certificate. The website provides the [questions](/assessment/final) and [practical case](/projects/capstone); DWIHN must approve the formal delivery platform, pass rule, retake process, attendance requirement, and certificate issuer.

## Outcomes and preparation

Explain the safe-use routine, complete the knowledge assessment, demonstrate a practical data-to-decision workflow, and identify where to seek help after the course. Review your S4 artifact, S7 report, S8 call summary, S10 brief, and S11 incident record.

## Session flow

Plan for at least one hour, including review, assessment, practical evaluation, and Q&A. The secure assessment may need a separate window when accommodations or testing conditions require more time.

| Sequence | Activity | Learner evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome; explain assessment conditions and access needs | Learners know the process |
| 2 | Rapid review of Classify, Confirm, Review | Verbal reasoning |
| 3 | Individual 20-question knowledge assessment | Answer sheet |
| 4 | Practical case: tool path, analytics interpretation, output review, incident first steps | Scored worksheet |
| 5 | Debrief and feedback on critical safety steps | Corrected reasoning |
| 6 | Dedicated Q&A; assign unresolved policy or tenant questions | Questions answered or routed |
| 7 | Close and explain approved certificate follow-up | Completion next step |

## Practical evaluation

The [capstone](/projects/capstone) combines classification, tool selection, Lumenore interpretation, communication review, and incident response. A good submission gives enough evidence that another reviewer can follow the decision. The instructor uses the rubric in that page and the private answer key in the source repository.

### Proposed pass rule

The course proposes **16/20 or higher** on the knowledge questions, plus all **critical safety steps** in the practical case: classify the data, confirm the authorized destination or stop, reject an unsupported AI claim, avoid disclosing a small cell, and report a possible exposure promptly. DWIHN may revise this standard. A missed critical step leads to feedback and a new practice case before a certificate is issued.

## After the course

Keep the [daily checklist](/resources/daily-checklist), [prompt builder](/resources/prompt-builder), [analytics review card](/resources/analytics-checklist), and [incident card](/resources/incident-card). Tool catalogs, policies, and product features change; check current versions during real work. Bring unresolved questions to your manager, privacy/security contact, data owner, or AI governance channel as applicable.
