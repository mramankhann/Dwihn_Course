# Month 1 · AI Foundations & Safe Usage

**Weeks 1–3 · Sessions 1–4 · Modules 1–5**

## Goal

Build a usable mental model of AI and a consistent safety routine before introducing live workflow tools. The order is deliberate: learners first understand likely errors, then permissions and data, then prompt design, then work application.

## Practical milestone

Use the [prompt builder](/resources/prompt-builder) to create a draft from synthetic material. Submit the original prompt, draft, review notes, corrected version, and a statement of which approved destination would be needed for real data. The instructor scores **safety first**, then utility and clarity.

## Expected outcome

Learners should stop unsafe data movement, improve a vague prompt, and explain what they personally checked in an AI output. A learner who cannot identify the approved tool or data tier has a clear next action: ask the DWIHN owner before using a live record.

## Month 1 review

Explain the handbook's five habits in one short case: **classify before acting, use approved tools, give clear instructions, verify before use, and keep humans accountable**. Submit the S4 artifact with a source check and one unresolved policy question routed to its owner.
