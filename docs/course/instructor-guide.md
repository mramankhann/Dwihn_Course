# Instructor guide

This guide supports delivery of the 12 Teams sessions. Plan for **at least one hour per session, including Q&A**, and extend the live session when learners need more explanation, practice, or discussion. The DWIHN and Netlink owners must confirm operational details before invitations are sent.

The supplied DWIHN AI Training Handbook is the teaching source for the ten [module pages](/course/month-1). Assign the relevant module before class. In the live session, use the module's detailed explanations and practice case as the content spine; use the session page for the teaching flow, demonstration, Q&A, and work-product instructions. Session 2 covers Modules 2 and 3, Sessions 5–7 divide Module 6, and Sessions 9–10 divide Module 8.

## Preparation checklist

| When | Instructor action |

| Before S11 | Confirm reporting channel, immediate containment instructions, escalation roles, evidence handling, and any internal timing standard. |
| Before S12 | Approve assessment pass criteria, accommodations, retakes, attendance, certificate wording, and issuer. |

## Standard facilitation pattern

Every session is planned for **at least one hour, including Q&A and close**. The session pages list the teaching sequence without assigning fixed time blocks. Assign module reading before class so the live session can focus on explanation, demonstration, learner practice, the knowledge check, and questions. Session 2 covers two modules; learners can review both module pages and the data desk card before class. Continue the session longer when questions or guided practice call for it.

1. **Open.** State the decision and the learning outcome; invite a quick retrieval response.
2. **Explain.** Teach one or two ideas with a workplace example; use the module page for detail.
3. **Demonstrate.** Narrate safety and validation checks with synthetic material.
4. **Practice.** Ask every learner to produce a small work product; invite one learner to explain a different choice.
5. **Check and answer.** Use the knowledge check to correct a misconception, then invite Q&A and route unresolved policy or configuration questions to the appropriate DWIHN owner.

## Facilitation notes by session

| Session | Demonstration | Likely misconception to address |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Compare a strong draft and a confident false claim | Fluent wording is not evidence. |
| S2 | Trace a synthetic member summary through classification and the live catalog | A product name alone is not approval. |
| S3 | Improve a vague prompt with five parts | Longer prompts are not automatically better. |
| S4 | Edit an AI-generated letter with a review log | A polished draft is not ready to send. |
| S5 | Ask a scoped KPI question, then inspect source and filters | A chart title does not define a measure. |
| S6 | Filter a dashboard by ZIP and compare denominator and small cells | A darker map color is not proof of a service gap. |
| S7 | Create a proposed alert and an annotated board report | Alerts require owners, thresholds, and response plans. |
| S8 | Correct a synthetic transcript/summary and choose escalation | Sentiment is a signal, not a diagnosis or triage decision. |
| S9 | Reconcile two apparently conflicting rates | Different denominators or date ranges can both be valid. |
| S10 | Chair a short data decision meeting | Forecasts do not remove human accountability. |
| S11 | Run a five-step incident tabletop | The proposal's 24-hour target does not replace immediate internal reporting. |
| S12 | Administer knowledge and practical checks | Certificate criteria need DWIHN approval. |

## Records, accessibility, and support

- Obtain permission to record and follow DWIHN's retention and access rules. Keep real member data out of demonstration recordings.
- Provide captioning, readable materials, alt text, and a text alternative for each diagram. Permit written or spoken responses where assessment rules allow.
- The proposal plans a support Teams channel and recordings. The program owner confirms the channel, moderation, response target, and recording location.
- Use `instructor/assessment-key.md` from the source repository. It is intentionally outside the published VitePress site.

## Feedback and remediation

For each practice task, give feedback on **permission and data**, **factual verification**, **human judgment**, and **clarity**. A missed critical safety step triggers coaching and a new case before certification. Capture repeated catalog or tool-access questions for the governance owner; do not silently turn an instructor guess into policy.
