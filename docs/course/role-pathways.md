# Role pathways

Every learner completes the core sequence. During S3, S4, S8, and S10, choose the case closest to your work. The examples below are proposed training workflows, not confirmation that a product feature is enabled for your role.

| Role | Main practice | Review before use | Especially relevant sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Care coordinator | Draft a member-facing letter or summarize a **synthetic** case | Identity, care facts, plain language, clinical sign-off, approved data route | S2–S4, S5–S7 |
| Program manager | Ask about service trends; create a dashboard and weekly report | Metric definition, denominator, date range, subgroup size, data freshness | S5–S7, S9–S10 |
| Access call center | Review a synthetic transcript, suggested article, and summary | Caller need, transcript errors, escalation, documentation rules | S2, S4, S8, S11 |
| Admin / operations | Draft a policy memo or recurring report | Current policy version, owner approval, audience, restricted content | S2–S4, S9 |
| Compliance / privacy | Classify data and rehearse incident triage | Scope, timestamps, evidence handling, policy owner, reporting route | S2, S4, S11 |
| Managers / leaders | Facilitate a data-driven team decision | Competing explanations, uncertainty, action owner, follow-up measure | S5–S7, S9–S11 |

## Transfer goal

At the end of the course, each learner should be able to teach a colleague the **Classify → Confirm → Review** routine and demonstrate one approved, role-relevant use with a traceable human decision.
