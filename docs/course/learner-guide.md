# Learner guide

## Before your first session

Read the [course overview](/course/overview) and keep the [daily AI checklist](/resources/daily-checklist) nearby. You do not need a paid AI account to complete this course. Product practice may use a trainer-led sandbox or screenshots if your DWIHN role does not have access.

## How to use each lesson

1. Begin with [Session 1](/sessions/s01-what-ai-is), then follow the numbered session links in order. Use the Month 1 and Month 2 module pages as deeper reading alongside the live sessions.
2. Read its **topic introduction** and four detailed points, pausing at each example to explain the safe decision in your own words.
3. Complete its **practical scenario**, **knowledge check**, and **action plan** using synthetic material.
4. Follow the demonstration and complete the guided practice on that session page; keep its Q&A and knowledge check with the session.
5. Keep the session work product and review notes; they become evidence for the final case.

The site is a learning resource. It does not grant permission to use a tool, upload records, share a dashboard, or issue a certificate.

## Practice safely

- Use only synthetic cases in class, recordings, and shared discussion unless DWIHN has explicitly authorized another training dataset.
- Do not paste a member record, call transcript, staff record, or credential into a public AI service.
- A de-identified example still needs a privacy review: unusual combinations or small groups can reveal a person.
- If your role lacks an approved tool or feature, analyze the example on paper and explain the decision you would make.
- Report a suspected mistake promptly via current DWIHN channels. Do not hide it or attempt an unapproved cleanup.

## If you miss a live session

The proposal plans Teams recordings and LMS materials. Your trainer will confirm where recordings are stored, who may view them, and when they are available. Read the session page, complete the practice and check, then bring one question to the next Q&A or approved support channel.

## What counts as a good answer

Good work names the **purpose**, **data tier**, **approved destination**, **source of facts**, **human review**, and **next action**. “The AI said so” is never enough evidence. If something is unknown, state the uncertainty and identify who can resolve it.
