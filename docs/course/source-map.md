"Requires clarification" means the teaching explanation is present but a current DWIHN policy, contact, catalog entry, tenant feature, or certificate rule is still needed for operational use.

## Consolidations and additions

- Modules 2 and 3 share S2. Module 6 spans S5-S7. Module 8 spans S9-S10, following the proposal table and handbook course map.
- The handbook repeats the same five general application steps in many points. The module pages preserve the safe decision sequence but replace repetition with topic-specific explanation and examples.
- The site adds a synthetic dataset, checklists, instructor answer key, and practical capstone so learners can demonstrate application without live member data.
- The proposal's partnership and promotional pages are background, not learner competencies. They inform attribution but are not taught as operational facts.
