# Month 2 · Advanced AI & Lumenore Mastery

**Weeks 4–9 · Sessions 5–12 · Modules 6–10**

## Goal

Move from safe individual use to evidence-based team work: query governed data, review product AI signals, decide with uncertainty, and respond to possible incidents.

## Practical milestone

The [capstone case](/projects/capstone) asks a team to choose a safe tool path, interpret a synthetic ZIP-level service-gap signal, draft a recommended action, and respond when a summary has been shared to the wrong location. It joins analytics, human review, and governance without using live member data.

## Expected outcome

Learners can explain where a number came from, what it does and does not prove, who owns a decision, and which internal process applies when a possible exposure occurs.

## Month 2 review

Use the [capstone case](/projects/capstone) to join the handbook's full decision sequence: task, information, classification, approved tool, prompt, verification, human action, and incident response. Complete the individual assessment under approved program rules.
